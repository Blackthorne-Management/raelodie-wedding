Questions & Answers

What's the dress code?

Garden formal. Think polished and elegant, with a fun, whimsical, tropical twist. Dressy, but make it joyful. Please, nothing mini, casual wear, jeans, or sneakers.

Is there a color scheme for guests?

Yes! Coral, blush, peach, champagne, or white/ivory (really, it's ok — you can wear white, just dress it up). Please no emerald green, dark colors (including black), or neons.

What should the gentlemen wear?

Light neutral suits — beige, tan, or sand.

What should the ladies wear?

A cocktail dress, midi, or gown all work beautifully. We all know how much we love it when people dress to impress! Warm tropical tones (see above) or white/ivory.

Can I bring a guest?

Our RSVP page will let you know whether a plus-one or family is included. If we left out a spouse or a dependent, please let us know ASAP.

What can we do at The Grove?

There are free shuttles to Disney for your convenience. There's also an on-site water park — we recommend renting a cabana for the day. More ideas on our Things To Do page.

Are pets allowed at the resort?

Unfortunately, no. We wish! The Grove Resort does not permit pets, so please plan to leave your furry friends at home.

Are you registered anywhere / what about gifts?

Yes — we're registered at Zola. Visit our Registry page for the link. If you'd rather, a contribution to our honeymoon fund would also mean the world.

Who do I reach out to with questions?

Check out The Crew! You can bother any of the wedding party, or our parents, for more information.
