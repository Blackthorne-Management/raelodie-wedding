Questions & Answers

What's the Dress Code?

May 7 · Island Glam

Island Glam is for Friday's Welcome Reception. Think upscale resort wear but fun! Don your satins, silks, and metallics! Barefoot is welcome, or wear shoes you don't mind getting sandy.

May 8 · Garden Formal

Garden Formal is Saturday for the Wedding Ceremony. Think polished and elegant, with a fun, whimsical, tropical twist. Dressy, but make it joyful. Please, nothing mini, casual wear, jeans, or sneakers.

Is There a Color Scheme for Guests?

May 7 · Island Glam

Any color goes!

May 8 · Garden Formal

Yes! Coral, blush, peach, champagne, or white/ivory (really, it's ok, you can wear white, just dress it up). Please no emerald green, dark colors (including black), or neons.

What Should the Gentlemen Wear?

May 7 · Island Glam

Linen trousers and floral or crochet shirts. Don't be afraid to show some chest hair!

May 8 · Garden Formal

Light neutral suits in beige, tan, or sand.

What Should the Ladies Wear?

May 7 · Island Glam

Upscale resort wear, but fun! Satins, silks, and metallics are all welcome, in any color you love.

May 8 · Garden Formal

A cocktail dress, midi, or gown all work beautifully. We all know how much we love it when people dress to impress! Warm tropical tones (see above) or white/ivory.

Can I Bring a Guest?

Our RSVP page will let you know whether a plus-one or family is included. If we left out a spouse or a dependent, please let us know ASAP.

What Can We Do at The Grove Resort?

There are free shuttles to Disney for your convenience. There's also an on-site water park, and we recommend renting a cabana for the day. More ideas on our Things To Do page.

Are Pets Allowed at the Resort?

Unfortunately, no. We wish! The Grove Resort does not permit pets, so please plan to leave your furry friends at home.
