Make it a trip

Things To Do

Come early, stay late, and turn our wedding weekend into your Florida vacation. Feel free to arrive before May 6 and head home after May 9 if you'd like more time in the sun.

On-site

Water Park Day

The Grove Resort has its own water park right on property. Our recommendation: grab a cabana for the day, park yourself in the shade, and let someone else fetch the drinks.

Nearby

Disney Shuttles

Free shuttles run from the resort straight to Walt Disney World, so a park day is easy to squeeze in without renting a car.

May 6

Fishing Expedition

Our parents are hoping to organize a fishing trip on Thursday, May 6. If that sounds like your kind of morning, let us know — reach out through The Crew page and we'll get you the details.

Where to eat

Dining, Elodie-approved

A few picks in the spirit of getelodofthis.com — polished but not precious, worth dressing up for.

Worth the drive

Harlow Grove

Award-winning small plates, large plates, and craft cocktails in downtown Winter Garden — the kind of place that shows up on "best of Orlando" lists for good reason.

Worth the drive

Rosallie Le French Cafe

All-day brunch modeled on the South of France, with a pastry case that photographs as well as it tastes.

Worth the drive

Urban on Plant Kitchen & Bar

Modern American on Plant Street with a stylish room and outdoor seating — flatbreads, salads, and a good cocktail list.

Easy & affordable

Three Birds Cafe

A cozy downtown Winter Garden cafe, great for a low-key breakfast or lunch without the wait or the price tag.

Kosher & affordable

Kosher Grill Orlando

Glatt kosher, Rabbi-supervised, and easy on the wallet — a solid option if you're keeping kosher for the weekend.

For the family crew

Family-friendly to-dos

West Orange Trail

22 miles of paved, shaded trail — rent bikes nearby and make an easy morning of it with the kids.

Winter Garden Farmers Market

Saturdays, 8am–1pm in the Downtown Pavilion — produce, baked goods, and local crafts in a walkable historic downtown.

Chapin Station

A local park with playgrounds and open space to let little ones burn off energy between events.

For the Shabbat crew

Synagogues in the area

Heads up: the resort itself is out near Disney, so nothing is truly within walking distance — the options below are all a drive away. If a group wants to coordinate a ride or carpool for Friday night or Saturday services, reach out through The Crew and we'll help organize it.

Temple Shalom of Central Florida

Reform congregation in the greater Orlando area.

Southwest Orlando Jewish Congregation

Ohalei Rivka — Orthodox congregation serving West Orlando/Horizons West.

Chabad of South Orlando

Services and community programming; a good first call for Shabbat arrangements while you're in town.
