Flying in

Getting From the Airport

The closest airport is Orlando International Airport (MCO), about a 30-minute drive from the resort.

Rideshare

Uber and Lyft both serve MCO and the resort. If you'd like an option that puts more of your fare directly in a driver's pocket, consider Empower, a driver-owned rideshare app where drivers set their own rates and keep 100% of what riders pay, instead of a large cut going to a corporate middleman. It's a small way to support fairer pay while you get where you're going.

Guests can use the code 2N7XMJ to get a discount off of their first ride!

Car Rental

All major rental companies operate out of MCO. If you're staying at the resort all weekend and plan to use the Disney shuttles, you may not need a car at all, but one gives you the most flexibility for exploring.
